# Synthetic PDS extract (100,000 records)

`100000_synthetic_PDS_records_97series.xlsx` is a synthetic extract of 100,000 records with the schema of the
Personal Demographics Service (PDS), the national register of NHS patients in England. It was generated by
Nick Hunt at NHS England (release date 30 September 2025) and is shared here with the permission of NHS England.
It is the patient population used in the Observational Fidelity study.

## Disclaimer (as provided by NHS England)

> The data contained in this file is synthetic test data.
> The names contained are live-like in appearance but are not names of real people.
> Any correspondence to the names of real people is entirely accidental.
> The addresses are valid addresses based on publicly available address data.
> GP and GP Practice data is either generated synthetic test data and/or based on publicly available data.

## Contents

| Sheet | Contents |
|---|---|
| `ReadMe` | Description, release date, author and the disclaimer above |
| `100000_synthetic_PDS_records_97` | 100,000 records, 18 columns: `UNIQUE_ID`, `NHS_NO`, `DOB`, `DOD`, `FAMILY_NAME`, `GIVEN_NAME`, `OTHER_GIVEN_NAME`, `TITLE`, `GENDER`, `ADDR1`–`ADDR5`, `POST_CODE`, `SENSITIVE_FLAG`, `GPP`, `SCENARIO` |
| `SQL` | The query used to export the records |

All NHS numbers in the file begin with 97 (the "97 series" of this test extract). Dates are in DD/MM/YYYY format.

## Use in the Observational Fidelity study

- Fields used: `GIVEN_NAME`, `FAMILY_NAME`, `DOB`, `GENDER` and `POST_CODE`. NHS number was not used.
- 1,341 records that are software test cases rather than plausible people were excluded (names containing
  digits, hyphenated test codes or padding strings, and full names held by 20 or more records), leaving 98,659
  records. The exclusion rule is in the study code.
- Groups were defined by age in 2025 (under 30, 30-39, 40 or over).
